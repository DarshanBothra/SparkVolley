# How to play Spark Volley

Spark Volley is a **keyboard neon arcade** in the browser. There is nothing to install. Open the game URL on a **computer** (desktop or laptop).

Phones and tablets cannot play. If you open the link on a phone, you will see a desktop-only screen. The soundtrack can still play if you tap the screen.

## Open the game

1. Use a computer running Windows, macOS, or Linux.
2. Open **Chrome**, **Firefox**, **Safari**, or **Edge**.
3. Go to the game URL you were sent (a `vercel.app` link, GitHub Pages, or similar).
4. The first visit shows **rules** (how to play, how you score, combos, if you miss, then bonuses). Press **Space** or **Right** to turn the page. On the last page, press **Space** to continue. **Esc** cannot skip this.
5. Press **Space** on the title screen to start. Press **Space** again to serve the spark. **Esc** or **P** opens options (settings, rules, copy stats).

Your best score is saved in this browser on this computer.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Left arrow or A | Move paddle left (also previous rules page) |
| Right arrow or D | Move paddle right (also next rules page) |
| Up / Down | Move between settings |
| Space | Continue rules / serve / retry / resume from options |
| P or Esc | Open or close options (pauses the round). Esc does not skip first-load rules. |
| S | Settings (from options) |
| R | Rules (from options) |
| M | Mute / unmute music |
| N | Next soundtrack track |
| C | Copy your stats from options or after a round |

## How to play

- Volley the spark with the paddle. Thread it through the glowing **halo gates**.
- Missing the spark ends the round.
- Thread a gate before the next paddle hit to keep your combo.
- Capsules sometimes fall from a gate. Catch them with the paddle:
  - **Twin Spark** — a second spark. Keep either one alive.
  - **Slow Field** — the spark eases for a few seconds.
  - **Lock Gates** — new hoops still appear, but they stay put.
  - **Aegis** — the floor saves a miss for a few seconds.

## Share

Anyone with the URL can play. They do not need an account. Send the link in chat, email, or a post. They must open it on a computer.
